| D-031 | Validation closure policy — exhaustive findings, bounded blockers (Codex workflow audit 2026-09-16). Verifiers attempt to FALSIFY the frozen contract; they do not search indefinitely for any reason to reject. All meaningful observations are reported — no severity filter on discovery — but a finding blocks the increment only if it satisfies the WHOLE blocking predicate: (1) maps to a criterion frozen before dispatch or an applicable repo-wide invariant, (2) reproduces through the exact production consumer/path at the PIN (not a surrogate alone), (3) materially changes output/state/evidence/determinism/integration behaviour (style, optional hardening, hypothetical misuse, defence-in-depth do not qualify by themselves), (4) has a concrete discriminator (deterministic repro / red control / valid mutation), (5) the fix is inside the component boundary. A statically suspected issue is `UNVERIFIED`/`FOLLOW-UP` unless the contract explicitly requires the static property; follow-ups do NOT open repair rounds. The verify lane produces two outputs — a FINDING INVENTORY (`BLOCKER`/`FOLLOW-UP`/`INFO`/`UNVERIFIED`) and a GATE RECOMMENDATION (`MERGE-READY` / `MERGE-READY-WITH-FOLLOWUPS` / `NOT-READY` / `CONTRACT-INVALID`); the coordinator owns the final gate. The repair budget is ONE focused repair by default (`build → independent verify → one focused repair → reverify`); a second only with explicit coordinator authorization when the first repair introduced or failed to close a qualifying blocker; no automatic third wave. The budget is keyed by `component/proof ID + frozen contract revision + production-code digest` and does NOT reset on a renamed wave, a new verifier, report/test/mutation/transcript/ledger/doc-only commits, a process-policy edit, or rediscovery of the same defect under a new label — a new budget needs a material production change or a recorded contract amendment; exhaustion is an honest BLOCKED decision for human prioritization, never a round-one restart. Escape hatch: a new exact-production-path defect that falsifies evidence, corrupts state, loses data, or causes destructive effects is `CONTRACT-DEFECT` — reproduced and returned to the coordinator for an explicit amendment, never a self-started wave. Workload routing: pure logic → TDD + focused tests + normal review; integration/stateful boundary → + one focused independent contract verification; proof/evidence/minting/external venue → the full proof workflow. | The build-verify loop was looping: "make the change fail" + "report EVERYTHING, no floor" + a binary ready/not-ready verdict + a round cap that reset under a renamed wave meant marginal, speculative, out-of-scope and proof-of-proof findings automatically reopened completed work (the round-level generalization of AF-AP-76). This keeps the verifier's adversarial strength and exhaustive reporting while removing exhaustive BLOCKING. Wired canonical-first into `.claude/agents/adversarial-verifier.md` + `harness-ports/roles/adversarial-verifier.md` (→ `.codex/agents/*` via build-roles.py), skills `contract-gate`/`build-loop`/`orchestration`/`anti-hollow-green`, `harness-ports/briefs/verify-contract.md`, and the three instruction mirrors; no proof, ledger, production component, active lane, remote workflow, or live service changed. |
