The proof machinery is built and adversarially graded. The central finding is that S0-02's five
negative classes are **not all decided by buzz-acp**: at the pinned commit `1c8321cd`, buzz-acp
decides only REPLAY and SELF-AUTHORED for an ordinary kind-9 channel event; SIGNATURE, MEMBERSHIP
and FRESHNESS are decided by buzz-relay at publish, so those events never reach buzz-acp and no
buzz-acp log line for them can exist. The seed's "four DISTINCT reasons" rule is still
**satisfiable — five distinct observables exist** — but only because the checker reads BOTH the
buzz-acp debug log AND the relay's delivery receipt. Three DISCREPANCIES are stated in full below.

A second, hard blocker is named rather than routed around: the two buzz-acp-decided legs need
`RUST_LOG=debug`, and the S0-01 launcher cannot pass it. The PC runner refuses those legs and says
why.
