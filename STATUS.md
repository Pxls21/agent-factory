  increment is pushed to the working branch after its gates pass, and the Stage 0 work awaits a new PR.
- No production container image or Compose deployment has been built.
- No Hermes Fubuki or composite ai-memory adapter has been implemented.
- No policy decision service has been implemented.
- No JIT Foundry, GBrain dream worker, evaluation runner, or HarnessRouter integration has been implemented.
- No provider credentials, relay keys, or production secrets have been configured in this repository; the owner's PC holds the
  running OmniRoute, Buzz relay, Ollama, Phoenix, OpenObserve and neo4j, reached over the token-gated bridge.
- No containment test of this project's containers has run; runsc itself is verified on both venues.
- S0-01's first end-to-end captures ran on the PC (2026-09-05/06) and exposed the checker's hollow acceptance; the proof is not minted.
